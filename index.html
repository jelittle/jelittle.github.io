I am a Master's student in Computer Science at York University, working on computer vision and computational photography. My current research is on generative models for video, with a focus on motion prediction and intuitive physics.

I am a research assistant with Prof. Shweta Mahajan at York University. Previously I worked with Prof. Michael S. Brown on color constancy and in-camera color reproduction. I completed my BSc in Computer Science (Specialized Honours) at York in 2026.
